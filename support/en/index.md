## FAQ

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open **Settings > your name (Apple ID) > Subscriptions > Mono** to cancel or change it. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. After you cancel, you can keep using it until the paid period ends.

### Can I get a refund?
Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### I switched devices and my entries are gone
If you linked Apple, Google, or Kakao, link the same account on the new device and your entries will come back. If you used Mono as a guest and deleted the app, the entries cannot be recovered, so link an account early if you plan to keep using Mono.

### How do I delete my account and entries?
Go to **Settings > Account** in the app and delete your account. The account is locked immediately, and the account and all entries on our server are deleted after 7 days. This cannot be undone.

### How do I remove ads?
Subscribe to Mono Premium. While subscribed, the app does not request ads.

### How do I use collage backgrounds and stickers?
Choose Collage from the add (+) screen. The Basic series is free, and all other series are available to Mono Premium subscribers.
